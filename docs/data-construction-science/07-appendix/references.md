# 参考文献

## 经典论文

### 数据质量
1. **Learning with Noisy Labels**
   - Frénay, B., & Verleysen, M. (2014). IEEE TNNLS
   - 噪声标签学习综述

2. **Confident Learning: Estimating Uncertainty in Dataset Labels**
   - Northcutt, C., et al. (2021). JAIR
   - 置信学习,估计数据集标签噪声

3. **A Survey on Data Collection for Machine Learning**
   - Chen, X., et al. (2021). arXiv
   - 机器学习数据采集综述

### 数据增强
4. **EDA: Easy Data Augmentation**
   - Wei, J., & Zou, K. (2019). EMNLP
   - 简单有效的NLP数据增强

5. **AutoAugment: Learning Augmentation Strategies**
   - Cubuk, E. D., et al. (2019). CVPR
   - 自动学习数据增强策略

6. **SpecAugment: A Simple Data Augmentation Method for ASR**
   - Park, D. S., et al. (2019). INTERSPEECH
   - 语音识别数据增强

### 主动学习
7. **Active Learning Literature Survey**
   - Settles, B. (2009). Computer Sciences Technical Report
   - 主动学习综述

8. **Deep Active Learning: Unified and Principled Method**
   - Gal, Y., & Ghahramani, Z. (2016). ICML Workshop
   - 深度主动学习

### 知识图谱
9. **Knowledge Graphs**
   - Hogan, A., et al. (2021). ACM Computing Surveys
   - 知识图谱综述

10. **BERT: Pre-training for NLP**
    - Devlin, J., et al. (2019). NAACL
    - 预训练模型,间接支持数据构建

   - 人机协作机器学习

4. **Building Machine Learning Powered Applications**
   - Ameisen, E. (2020)
   - ML应用构建,含数据章节
